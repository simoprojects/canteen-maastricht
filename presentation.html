The problem

Lunch break is short.
The canteen makes it shorter.

  • No free seatsAt peak time, thousands of students look for a table at the same moment.
  • Hidden allergensAllergens and nutrition values are hard to find at the counter.
  • Shared discountsA screenshot of a student card works for anyone.
  • No say in the menuStudents can't suggest what the canteen cooks.

1:40 – 2:40 · Four problems. One: no seats at peak time. Two: if you have an allergy, you have to ask at the counter. Three: discounts can be shared with a screenshot. Four: students have no voice in the menu.

The solution

One app for three canteens

    • Book a seat for up to 30 minutes, check in with the QR on your table
    • Order takeaway, pay in the app, pick it up in a canteen box
    • One weekly menu with photos, allergens and nutrition values
    • Everything in English and Dutch

    2:40 – 3:30 · The app covers FASoS, Law and SBE: 7,500 seats in total. You choose a canteen, then either book a seat or order takeaway. The menu is the same everywhere, with every allergen and nutrition value.
